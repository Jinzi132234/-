## Recurring grammar

Palette. Full-bleed stages rotate through terracotta orange, emerald green, cobalt blue, violet, hot pink, cream, navy, and peach. The same six sticker colors repeat on tiles: blue, pink, gold, green, purple, orange. Neutrals are cream cards and near-black windows. Headlines are cream or black with one word punched in gold or coral. The host shot is a real office with a purple practical light, used as a cutaway, not a keyed background.

Cards. Chunky and borderless: large corner radius, generous padding, soft drop shadow. Two-part tiles (saturated top, cream body) are the default. Hero tiles sometimes sit a few degrees off-axis. The lesson layout wraps everything in a thick white rounded board on a blue-to-teal gradient. macOS windows are charcoal, with traffic lights, a small coral asterisk in the title, and an inner pill or orange-stroked field.

Pills. Fully rounded capsules, two families. Cream/white kickers in small caps ("THEY CALL IT", "IN THIS VIDEO", "JUST DROPPED", "THE REAL JOB"). Saturated status pills in black, green, red, gold, or blue, often with a tiny icon (toggle, Install, Run, "SEE WHAT'S RUNNING").

Layouts, in a loop.
- Full face, then hard cut to a full stage, then back. Not a slow dissolve.
- Signature lesson board: gradient frame + white rounded stage + host PiP, rounded rect, bottom-left, overlapping the board.
- Split: face on one side, one big card on the other (sometimes a neon vertical rule).
- One idea per stage. Headline, then 1–6 cards, lots of empty margin.

Motion feel. Snappy and sticker-like. Words and letter-tiles land one at a time (about a third of a second apart); late tiles tilt as they settle. Stacked cards build top to bottom, then lock into a grid. On the face, a big cream card pops in on the left and a second card stacks under it while a numbered list reveals line by line. UI mocks type, progress bars fill, and a green "done" pill pops. Filmstrips slide sideways; extra windows fan out with offset. Almost no camera move. The train photos are content inside this system (badge + filmstrip), not a different look.
